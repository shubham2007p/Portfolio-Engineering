# Privacy Policy & Data Transparency — Shubham Panwar Portfolio

This privacy policy explains how data is handled across the Shubham Panwar Engineering Portfolio (`https://shubham2007p.github.io/Portfolio-Engineering`) and its associated backend API services.

## 1. Information Collection & Use
- **Contact Forms & API Submissions**: When you send an email or submit a contact inquiry via `/api/contact`, your name, email address, and message contents are processed solely to respond to your request. No contact information is sold or shared with third-party advertisers.
- **Interactive Chatbot (`/api/chat`)**: Chat prompts submitted to the chatbot terminal are processed to answer queries regarding Shubham's work, experience, and projects. No personal identifying information is permanently attached to chat queries.
- **Analytics & Logs**: Server logs collect standard operational metrics (IP addresses, request paths, user agents, HTTP response status) strictly for security monitoring and system performance auditing.

## 2. AI Agent Crawling Policy
This site welcomes AI crawlers, search indexing agents, and LLM assistants. AI User-Agents (`ChatGPT-User`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`, `DeepSeekBot`, `PerplexityBot`, etc.) are granted open access to inspect static pages, markdown resources (`/llms.txt`), and OpenAPI specifications.
